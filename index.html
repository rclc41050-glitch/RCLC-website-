About RCLC

The Rohingya Community Learning Centre (RCLC) serves Rohingya refugee children who have extremely limited access to education. Because many refugee children in Malaysia are unable to enroll in formal schools, RCLC provides a safe and stable environment where children can learn, grow, and build hope for the future.

Current Situation

The current learning centre operates from a single apartment. Around 60–90 students come to the centre each weekday from 5–7 PM to learn English and mathematics.

A Safe Place to Learn

Many students come from difficult circumstances, including conflict, displacement, loss, and unstable caregiving situations. For these children, the learning centre is more than a school. It is a place of safety and care.

Mission & Goals

Our Mission

To provide quality education, character development, and a safe learning environment for refugee children who lack access to formal schooling.

Short-Term Goals

Strengthen students' literacy, numeracy, English proficiency, and basic life skills.

Long-Term Goals

Help students grow in knowledge, character, leadership, and confidence so they are better equipped for future opportunities.

Education can help students navigate life more successfully in Malaysia, communicate more effectively within the broader community, and prepare for possible resettlement, higher education, or future employment opportunities.

Curriculum & Programs

RCLC plans to utilize a Cambridge-based curriculum that provides students with a structured and internationally recognized academic foundation. The program will be adapted to students' different ages, educational backgrounds, and learning levels.

🇬🇧

English

Reading, writing, speaking, listening, vocabulary, and communication skills.

🔢

Mathematics

Numeracy, mathematical understanding, practical skills, and problem-solving.

🔬

Science

Age-appropriate learning about the natural world through lessons and activities.

🌱

Life Skills

Communication, cooperation, responsibility, decision-making, and problem-solving.

🌟

Character Development

Leadership, personal responsibility, confidence, and positive relationships.

🤝

Social-Emotional Growth

Supporting confidence, communication, teamwork, and healthy relationships.

Beyond Academics

In addition to academics, the program will emphasize leadership development, personal responsibility, communication skills, and social-emotional growth to help students flourish both inside and outside the classroom.

Target Population

RCLC will primarily serve Rohingya refugee children living within our community in Meru.

Age Group

The program will focus on students approximately 6 to 16 years old.

Expected Students

Initially, we expect to serve between 80 and 90 students, with potential for future growth as the program expands and additional resources become available.

Our Goal for Students

To help students learn, grow in confidence, develop essential skills, and prepare for future educational and life opportunities.

Teachers & Volunteers
